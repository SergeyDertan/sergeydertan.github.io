# Multitool help & support

## Contact

In the app, tap Settings → Send feedback: the app version is added for you. Or write to [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}), and please include the app version (Settings → About & support the developer), your phone model and what happened. I read every message.

## Questions

### Is Multitool free?
Yes. The everyday tools are free, complete and without ads. Multitool Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks Sensor dashboard, GPS info, Network info and Dev utilities, and it supports the app's development.

### I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new phone?
Sign in to the store with the same Google account or Apple ID you used to buy it, then tap Settings → Restore purchases. On iPhone, Pro is also shared with your Family Sharing group.

Purchases stay within one store: Pro bought on Android doesn't carry over to an iPhone, and the other way round.

### Does the app need the internet?
No. Everything works offline except the public IP lookup and ping to addresses on the internet (ping to devices on your own network works without it), buying or restoring Pro, sending feedback, and opening links: from scanned codes, and the Help and Privacy pages.

### A tool says "Not available on your device"
Your phone doesn't have the sensor that tool needs, for example a magnetometer for the compass or a barometer for air pressure. The app can't replace missing hardware.

### I said no to a permission. How do I turn it back on?
Open the tool again and tap its Allow button. If the app shows Open system settings instead, tap it and allow the permission there. The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it, and everything else keeps working.

### The compass points the wrong way
Tap Calibrate in the compass and move the phone slowly in a figure 8. Keep away from metal, magnets and phone cases with magnets.

### The ruler or the level isn't accurate
Both can be calibrated. In the ruler, tap Calibrate, choose a bank card, a coin or a ruler, and resize the outline until it matches. In the level, put the phone on a surface you know is level and tap Calibrate.

### Are the sound meter readings exact?
No. Phone microphones aren't calibrated measuring devices, so readings are approximate. They are good for comparing places and noises, not for official measurements.

### The timer rang late or didn't ring
Allow notifications when the timer asks for them. On Android, also tap Allow alarms when asked, so the timer rings on time while the phone is idle. Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery: if the timer still doesn't ring, turn off battery optimization for Multitool in the system settings.

### What data does the app collect?
No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Crash reports help me fix errors and can be turned off in Settings → Send crash reports. Pro purchases are checked by RevenueCat, and on Android the QR scanner reads codes with Google's ML Kit, which sends Google technical data. Details are in the [privacy policy](/multitool/privacy/).

### How do I change the language?
Settings → Language. The app is available in English, Ukrainian and Russian.
