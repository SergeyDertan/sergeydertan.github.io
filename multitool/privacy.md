# Multitool privacy policy

- Contact: [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }})

This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your measurements, scans and inputs stay on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you do in the tools is processed on the device and is never sent to me or anyone else:

- **Camera** (QR scanner, magnifier): the picture is analysed live and is not saved or uploaded.
- **Photos** (Scan from a photo in the QR scanner): only the photo you pick is read, to find a code in it.
- **Microphone** (sound meter): the loudness is measured live. Nothing is recorded or stored.
- **Location** (GPS info, and the Wi-Fi name in Network info): used only while the tool is open, shown on screen, never stored or sent.
- **Motion and other sensors** (level, compass, sensor dashboard): read live, never stored or sent.
- **Notifications** (timer): used only to ring when a timer ends.
- **Text you type or paste** (dev utilities, QR generator, converters) and **codes you scan**: processed on the device only.

The app saves your settings, pinned tools and tool preferences (for example the ruler calibration) in the phone's app storage. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

The app asks for each permission only when you open a tool that needs it, and every tool that doesn't need a permission works without it. You can withdraw a permission at any time in the system settings.

## What leaves your phone

**Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google).** When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report so that I can fix the problem. A report contains technical information: the error and where in the code it happened, the device model, the operating system version, the app version, app settings such as theme and language, the name of the tool that was open, a short technical log of app events (for example "torch turned on"), and a random installation ID created by Crashlytics. Reports never contain what you scanned, typed, measured or where you are. Crash reports are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn them off in Settings → Send crash reports. See [Firebase's privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

**Purchases (RevenueCat).** Multitool Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the App Store. The app uses RevenueCat to check and restore the purchase. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and a random app user ID; it does not receive your name, email address or payment details, which stay with Google or Apple. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

**Network tools, only when you use them.**
- *Ping* sends network requests to the address you enter. That server sees your IP address, as with any connection.
- *Public IP address* in Network info asks the ipify service (api64.ipify.org) which address the internet sees. ipify learns your IP address. This happens only when you tap Look up.

The app does not use analytics, advertising IDs or any other tracking, and it does not sell or share your data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Your rights

Because I don't hold data that identifies you, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have a question or a request about crash reports or purchases, contact me at the address below and I will help.
